SMS Privacy Policy
Effective Date: October 19, 2026

Signature Wellness PA (“Signature Wellness,” “we,” “our,” or “us”) respects your privacy and is committed to protecting your personal information. This SMS Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect information related to our text messaging communications.

1. Information We Collect

When you opt in to receive SMS communications from Signature Wellness PA, we may collect:
- Your mobile phone number
- Your name and contact information
- Appointment and scheduling information
- Communication preferences
- Information you provide through text messaging interactions

2. How We Use SMS Information

We use SMS messaging to communicate with patients regarding:
- Appointment reminders
- Appointment confirmations and scheduling updates
- Responses to patient inquiries
- Customer care and support communications
- Administrative notifications related to patient services

SMS messages will not be used for promotional marketing unless separate consent is obtained where required by law.

3. Consent to Receive SMS Messages

By providing your mobile phone number and opting in, you consent to receive text messages from Signature Wellness PA. Consent is not a condition of receiving medical care or services.

Message frequency may vary depending on your interactions with our clinic.

4. Message and Data Rates

Message and data rates may apply depending on your mobile carrier and service plan. Signature Wellness PA is not responsible for any charges incurred from SMS messaging.

5. Opting Out

You may opt out of SMS communications at any time by replying:
- STOP to unsubscribe
- HELP for assistance

After opting out, you may receive a final confirmation message confirming your unsubscribe request.

6. Information Sharing and Disclosure

SMS and Consumer Data Sharing
We do not share SMS content or consumer data with third parties for marketing or unrelated business purposes. Consumer data, including mobile information, will not be transferred, shared, sold, or disclosed to external organizations except as required by law or to strictly necessary service providers acting on our behalf to support message delivery and related services.

Service Providers
Any service providers that receive consumer data on our behalf are authorized to use it only for the limited purpose of providing those services to us and are prohibited from using or disclosing it for any other purpose.

Contact Information
If you have questions about our messaging practices or this privacy policy, please contact the message sender using the contact information provided on the website or in the message you received.


7. Data Security

We implement reasonable administrative, technical, and physical safeguards designed to protect your personal information from unauthorized access, disclosure, or misuse.

However, no electronic transmission or storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

8. HIPAA and Healthcare Communications

As a healthcare provider, Signature Wellness PA takes patient privacy seriously. SMS communications may not always be fully secure. Patients should avoid sending highly sensitive medical information through text message.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this SMS Privacy Policy from time to time. Any updates will be posted on our website with a revised effective date.
